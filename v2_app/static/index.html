START SMALL

Your first session

A one-minute adult trial is a good place to begin.

01

Find your view

Choose Camera, then Find cameras. Select your built-in or USB camera. Keep the laptop steady, use good light, and make sure shoulders and hands are visible.

02

Try one minute

Select Low sensitivity and a one-minute trial. Confirm the adult-only trial, then start. Raise your hand three separate times, lowering it between raises.

03

Review what happened

Stop to see the recap. Check whether the counts reflect your trial. You can print or download the totals, or delete the recap.

A few things to know

What do the coloured cues mean?

Yellow means visible movement has continued; red means it has continued longer. High sensitivity shortens the wait. Cues do not assess attention, emotions, learning, or behaviour.

What is saved?

Only session totals and a room-level movement timeline. No images, video, names, face templates, or individual histories are saved. Recaps expire after 30 days and can be deleted sooner.

How do I hide the screen?

Press Escape or Hide screen to cover the entire dashboard immediately. Camera images and counters are blocked while hidden. Show dashboard brings it back. Closing the dashboard stops capture after 30 seconds.

The camera isn’t working

Close Zoom, Teams, FaceTime, and other camera apps. On Mac, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera and allow the app that launched Classroom Mirror (usually Terminal or Codex). On Windows, check Settings → Privacy & security → Camera and allow desktop apps. Reopen the launcher after changing permissions.

Can I use this with students?

This build is for consenting adult trials. The project’s design requires school approval and a data-protection review before classroom use. Crowding, distance, occlusion, and camera shake can change the results.
