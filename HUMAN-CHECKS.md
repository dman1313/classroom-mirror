# Your sign-off sheet — criteria 15 to 19

> **Historical V1 sign-off.** These Mac/LIMS/pose-only checks are retained as
> evidence and do not approve the accepted V2 product. The V2 adult-only Windows
> smoke is [WINDOWS-V2-SMOKE.md](WINDOWS-V2-SMOKE.md).

The automatic tests prove 14 of the contract's 19 criteria. These last five
are yours: things only a human can judge. Do them in one sitting (about ten
minutes). Adults only in front of the camera — never real pupils during
testing.

## Criterion 18 — it starts on your Mac
Double-click `run.command`. The browser opens the app.

## Criterion 16 — zone setup takes under a minute
Click "Start an individual session". Using only the on-screen hints: drag a
box over a chair, type `L-1` as the LIMS code, tick consent, press Start.
Time yourself.
☐ YES ☐ NO

## Criterion 15 — the live trial (you are the test child)
Sit in the chair you boxed. Slowly raise your hand high, hold it a second,
lower it. Do that **3 times**. The live view's counter should read 3 (within
one). Then walk out of the box for about a minute, come back, and Stop the
session. In the report, the "time at their spot" for that stretch should
clearly drop.
☐ YES ☐ NO

## Criterion 17 — reports make sense in under a minute
Open the demo report "LIMS L-7" from the home page, and one whole-class
summary. If a colleague with no tech background read them, would they get it
in a minute?
☐ YES ☐ NO

## Criterion 19 — you can run the proof yourself
In Terminal, in this folder, run `./check`. You should see the scoreboard
with the guardrails first.
☐ YES ☐ NO
